name: Reflow commit flow
description: The user's expected interaction for applying schedule recommendations in Reflow.
---

For Reflow, when the athlete says they like one specific recommendation, stage a pending plan card. Keep the schedule unchanged until they click **Reflow the week**. Use “Reflow” as the name of the explicit schedule action.

**Why:** The user chose a button as the final commit after conversational agreement.

**How to apply:** Keep brainstorming read-only, ask whether the athlete likes one specific recommendation, then stage a validated change and apply it only when they click the button.
